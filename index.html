FAQ

Which sizes does App Store Connect accept?

For iPhone you need the 6.9" slot (1320 × 2868). Apple generates the smaller sizes from it. 6.7" (1290 × 2796) and 6.5" (1242 × 2688) are still accepted if you already have them.

Does it crop my screenshot?

Only horizontally, by a few pixels (2–3 px), because the aspect ratios differ by under 1%. Nothing visible is lost. If the difference is larger than 10% the file is skipped.

Are my screenshots uploaded?

No. Everything happens in this tab with the Canvas API. You can even turn off Wi-Fi and it keeps working.

Can I use it on an iPhone?

Yes — tap the drop area to pick photos. Downloading several files at once needs a desktop browser; one at a time works everywhere.
